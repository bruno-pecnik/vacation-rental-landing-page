Surroundings

The apartment sits about 900 m from Babe Beach and Gaj Beach, within easy walking distance of Novalja's town centre (around 1.3 km). Rimski Kamenolom and the Sunčani Sat viewpoint are both close by. Zadar Airport is roughly 80 km away — ask us about arranging an airport transfer.

Novalja

Novalja is one of the island of Pag's liveliest coastal towns — known for its beaches, relaxed Adriatic atmosphere, and the famous Zrće Beach nightlife nearby. It's a great base for exploring the island's beaches, dry-stone walls, and local specialties like Pag cheese and lace.
