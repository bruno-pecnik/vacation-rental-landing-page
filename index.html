Location

Surroundings

The apartment sits about 900 m from Babe Beach and Gaj Beach, within easy walking distance of Novalja's town centre (around 1.3 km), and the Roman quarry just north of town is close by too. Two small shops and a bakery are a short walk away, in a quiet neighbourhood surrounded by many beautiful, upscale houses with pools and gardens. Zadar Airport is roughly 80 km away — ask us about arranging an airport transfer.

Who we are

This website does not collect or store personal data

This is a simple informational website with no backend server or database behind it. We don't ask you to create an account, we don't run analytics or advertising trackers, and we don't sell or share data with anyone — because none is collected here in the first place.

The contact form

The form on this page doesn't submit anything to us or to any server. When you fill it in and press “Send via WhatsApp”, your browser simply opens a pre-filled WhatsApp conversation addressed to us, using the text you typed. That message is then sent directly from your own device through WhatsApp, and is covered by WhatsApp's (Meta's) own privacy policy, not ours. We only see your message once you actually send it there.

Your language preference

Your choice of display language is remembered locally in your own browser (using what's called “local storage”), purely so the site opens in the right language next time. That stays on your device — it's never sent to us or to anyone else, which is also why this site has no cookie banner.

Embedded third-party content

This page loads two services from Google: Google Fonts (for the typefaces) and Google Maps (to show our location). Loading them can cause your browser to connect directly to Google's servers, which may process technical information such as your IP address under Google's own privacy policy. We also link out to Booking.com and to WhatsApp; once you follow one of those links, that site's own privacy policy applies instead of ours.

Your rights

Because we don't operate a database of personal data through this website, there is normally nothing here for us to access, correct or delete on your behalf. If you've messaged us directly (by WhatsApp, phone, or through Booking.com), you can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete that correspondence — just contact us using the details above. You can also lodge a complaint with Croatia's data protection authority, AZOP (Agencija za zaštitu osobnih podataka), at azop.hr.

Changes to this policy
